title: Welcome to Hardwire
---

Hardwire takes you from a digital logic design all the way to real, fabricated silicon.

This section covers everything you need to get started.

## What you'll need

- **Basic familiarity with Verilog or VHDL.** New to HDL? Start with the [Verilog / VHDL Primer](/dashboard/docs/getting-started/resources) before diving into T1.
- **A GitHub account.** Your submissions are scanned directly from a public repo, so you'll need somewhere to put your work.
- **A way to simulate your design.** Icarus Verilog is free and works well for T1 — you'll need this to generate the simulation output your submission requires.

## The tiers

You'll move through three tiers: **T1 (Digital Logic)**, **T2 (ASIC Tapeout)**, and **T3 (Custom Carrier Board)**. Each one unlocks once the previous is approved.
