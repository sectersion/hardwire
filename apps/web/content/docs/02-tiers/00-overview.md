Hardwire moves through three tiers, each building on the last. Complete one to unlock the next.

| Tier | Focus | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | Digital Logic | iCE40 FPGA Board |
| T2 | ASIC Tapeout | ASIC Shuttle Slot |
| T3 | Custom Carrier Board | PCB Fab & Test Components |

## How submission works

Every tier works the same way:

1. Push your work to a public GitHub repo linked to your project.
2. Go to the tier page and hit submit. We scan your repo automatically and show you what was found.
3. The scan is just a heads-up — it won't stop you from submitting, even if something wasn't detected.
4. A reviewer checks your submission and either approves it or requests changes.
5. If changes are requested, you'll see exactly what was said, right on the tier page, and you can resubmit.

You can only have one submission under review at a time per tier, and can't resubmit once a tier is already completed.
