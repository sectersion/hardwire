---
title: T1 Requirements
---

**Focus:** Design and simulate a digital logic circuit
**Reward:** iCE40 FPGA Board

To submit for T1, your repo should have:

- **RTL source files** - `.v`, `.sv`, `.vhd`, or `.vhdl`
- **A testbench** - same extensions as above, but the filename needs `tb`, `test`, or `testbench` somewhere in it so it's detected separately from your design files
- **Simulation output** - `.vcd`, `.fst`, `.ghw`, or `.wlf`
- **A `README.md`** explaining your design decisions

## A note on the automatic scan

When you submit, we scan your repo and show you what we found before you confirm. This is a heads-up, not a hard gate — if the scan misses something that's genuinely there (usually because of an unexpected filename), you can still submit. Just make sure a human reviewer can actually find and understand what you built.
